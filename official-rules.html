Blackbeard's Wake Leaderboard Contest

Official Rules

Last updated: 5/26/2026

No purchase necessary to enter or win. A purchase does not increase your chances of winning.

1. Sponsor

The Blackbeard's Wake Leaderboard Contest (the "Contest") is sponsored by Blackbeard World LLC ("Sponsor").

2. Eligibility

The Contest is open only to legal residents of the United States who are 18 years of age or older at the time of entry.

Employees, officers, and directors of Sponsor, and their immediate family members or members of the same household, are not eligible to participate.

Void where prohibited by law.

3. Contest Period

The Contest consists of recurring weekly leaderboard competitions:

  • Each Contest Period begins Monday at 12:00 AM CST and ends the following Sunday at 11:59 PM CST.

Sponsor's system time shall be the official timekeeping method.

4. How to Enter

Participants may enter by downloading and playing Blackbeard's Wake and achieving a score during the applicable Contest Period.

  • Scores are automatically recorded via the game's leaderboard system.
  • Each participant's highest score during the Contest Period will be used for ranking.
  • Participants may play an unlimited number of times during the Contest Period.
  • No purchase is required to participate.
  • Optional purchases (including ad removal) do not provide any gameplay or scoring advantage.

5. Winner Determination

Winners are determined based on the highest verified scores achieved during the Contest Period.

Each participant may win only one prize per Contest Period. If a participant achieves multiple qualifying scores, only their highest-ranking position will be awarded, and the next eligible participant will move up in ranking.

Weekly Prizes

  • 1st Place: $100
  • 2nd Place: $50
  • 3rd Place: $25

Tie-Breaker

In the event of a tie, the participant who achieved the tied score earliest during the Contest Period will receive the higher ranking.

All results are subject to verification by Sponsor. Sponsor can invalidate any score for any reason.

6. Winner Notification

Potential winners will be contacted via the email address associated with their account within a reasonable time after the end of the Contest Period.

To claim a prize, winners must:

  • Respond within 7 days of the initial notification.
  • Provide any required information for prize fulfillment.

If a winner does not respond within the required timeframe, is found ineligible, or fails to comply with these Official Rules, the prize may be forfeited and awarded to an alternate winner.

7. Prizes & Payment

Prizes will be awarded via Tremendous or another payout method selected by Sponsor.

  • Prizes are non-transferable and cannot be exchanged or substituted except at Sponsor's discretion.
  • Sponsor reserves the right to substitute a prize of equal or greater value if necessary.

8. Taxes

Winners are solely responsible for any applicable federal, state, or local taxes.

If required by law, Sponsor may request tax documentation (such as a W-9) prior to issuing payment.

9. Fair Play & Disqualification

This Contest is intended to reward skill-based gameplay.

Sponsor reserves the right to disqualify any participant who:

  • Cheats or attempts to manipulate gameplay or scoring.
  • Uses bots, automation, or unauthorized third-party tools.
  • Exploits bugs, glitches, or unintended mechanics.
  • Attempts to interfere with the integrity of the Contest.
  • Provides false or misleading information.

Sponsor may review gameplay data and invalidate scores that are deemed suspicious or unverifiable.

10. Limitation of Liability

By participating, entrants agree to release and hold harmless Sponsor from any liability arising from participation in the Contest or acceptance, use, or misuse of any prize.

Sponsor is not responsible for:

  • Technical failures, server issues, or interruptions.
  • Errors in score tracking, leaderboard display, or data transmission.
  • Lost, late, or misdirected communications.

11. Privacy

Information collected from participants will be used for the purposes of administering the Contest and awarding prizes.

A Privacy Policy will be made available prior to or at the time of account registration.

12. General Conditions

Sponsor reserves the right to modify, suspend, or terminate the Contest at any time for any reason, including if the Contest cannot be conducted as planned due to technical issues, fraud, or other causes beyond Sponsor's control.

Sponsor reserves the right to make changes to these Official Rules at any time. Continued participation constitutes acceptance of any updates.

All decisions by Sponsor are final and binding.

13. Governing Law

The Contest is governed by the laws of the State of Tennessee, without regard to conflict of law principles.

14. Contact

For questions regarding the Contest, contact: support@blackbeard.world
