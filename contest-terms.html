No purchase necessary to enter or win. A purchase does not increase your chances of winning.

2. Eligibility

The Contest is open only to legal residents of the United States who are 18 years of age or older at the time of participation.

You are not eligible to participate if:

  • You are under 18 years old.
  • You are not a legal resident of the United States.
  • You are located in a jurisdiction where participation is prohibited or restricted by law.
  • You are an employee, officer, contractor, or representative of Blackbeard World LLC, or an immediate family member or household member of any such person.

The Contest is void where prohibited or restricted by law.

3. Agreement Required for Prize Eligibility

To be eligible to win a Contest prize, you must:

  • Have a registered Blackbeard's Wake account.
  • Provide a valid email address.
  • Agree to these Contest Terms.
  • Comply with all gameplay, fair play, verification, and prize claim requirements.

Users who do not agree to these Contest Terms may still be able to play Blackbeard's Wake, but they will not be eligible to win Contest prizes.

4. Contest Period

The Contest is a weekly skill-based leaderboard contest.

Each weekly Contest ends on Sunday at 9:00 PM Central Time.

For clarity, "Central Time" means Central Time in the United States, observing CST or CDT as applicable.

Unless otherwise stated in the app, each new weekly Contest begins immediately after the prior weekly Contest ends.

Sponsor's servers and leaderboard system are the official timekeeping method for the Contest.

5. How to Enter

To enter, eligible users must play Blackbeard's Wake and submit a valid score during the applicable Contest Period.

There is no purchase required to participate.

Users may make an unlimited number of attempts before the Contest cutoff time. Only each user's highest verified score during the Contest Period will count toward prize eligibility.

Optional purchases, including ad removal, do not provide any gameplay advantage, scoring advantage, leaderboard advantage, or increased chance of winning.

6. Skill-Based Contest

The Contest is intended to reward skill-based gameplay.

Winners are determined based on the highest verified scores achieved during the Contest Period, subject to these Contest Terms and Sponsor's verification process.

Any random or variable gameplay elements, if present, are not intended to materially determine Contest outcomes.

7. Winner Determination

At the end of each Contest Period, the three highest verified eligible scores on the prize-eligible leaderboard will be selected as potential winners.

Weekly Prizes

  • 1st Place: $100
  • 2nd Place: $50
  • 3rd Place: $25

Each user may win only one prize per Contest Period. If a user has multiple scores on the leaderboard, only that user's highest verified score will count.

If there is a tie, the user who achieved the tied score earliest during the Contest Period will receive the higher ranking.

All scores and rankings are subject to review and verification by Sponsor before prizes are awarded.

Sponsor may require additional verification before awarding a prize.
